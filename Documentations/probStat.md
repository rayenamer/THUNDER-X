## AI Case Study: Natural Language to XPath System

### Background

Thunders scaling its AI-powered test automation platform. A critical component of our system needs to translate natural language testing instructions into precise XPath selectors that can reliably interact with web elements across diverse applications.

### The Challenge

Design and build a system that converts natural language commands into actionable XPath expressions that are valid against the current open page (for which you have access to HTML), along with a comprehensive evaluation strategy and deployment pipeline.

### Example of client instructions:

- "Click on 'about us'"
- "Hover over 'ok' under 'Employee' section"
- "Select the dropdown next to 'Country'"

### Your Mission

You need to present a comprehensive solution that demonstrates:

### 1. Technical Architecture (40% of evaluation)

- **System Design**: How would you architect this NL-to-XPath system?
- **AI/ML Approach**: What models, techniques, or frameworks would you use?

### 2. Evaluation Strategy (40% of evaluation)

- **Success Metrics**: How do you measure system performance?
- **Test Dataset**: What data would you use for training and/or evaluation?
- **Quality Assurance**: How do you ensure XPath reliability?
- **Continuous Improvement**: How do you detect and address model drift or failures?

### 4. Leadership & Strategy (20% of evaluation)

- **Stakeholder Communication**: How do you explain AI concepts to non-technical leadership?
- **Innovation Pipeline**: What adjacent AI opportunities do you see for Thunders ?
